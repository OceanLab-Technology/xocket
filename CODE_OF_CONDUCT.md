# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind and assuming good faith
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Saying "I don't know" and "I was wrong" when they are true

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@oceanlab.in**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not act in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions themselves.

Maintainers will respond with whatever is proportionate: a private warning, a
temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
